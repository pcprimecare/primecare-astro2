## Call 911 in an emergency

Call 911 in an emergency, when someone's life is in danger or they need urgent help.

## Call Health Link at 811 for non-emergency advice

Health Link 811 is an Alberta Health Services telephone service for non-emergency health advice.

## Call the clinic to book an appointment

To book an appointment at Primecare Medical Clinic, book online or call 403-398-5449 when the clinic is open. Online requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance. We do not use email to book appointments or answer medical questions.
