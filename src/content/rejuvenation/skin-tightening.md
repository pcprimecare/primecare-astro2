## What microneedling is

Microneedling uses very fine needles to make tiny punctures in the skin.

## What it is used for at the clinic

- Tighten and firm your skin
- Reduce blemishes
- Acne scars

## Coverage

Microneedling is an esthetic service and is not covered by Alberta Health Care Insurance (AHCIP).

## How to book

Use the rejuvenation online booking, or call 403-398-5449 when the clinic is open. Online requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance.
