## What laser is used for at the clinic

- Rosacea and redness
- Freckles and hyperpigmentation
- Cherry angiomas and skin tags
- Benign pigmented lesions
- Facial and leg spider veins
- Laser hair removal

## Coverage

Laser treatments are esthetic services and are not covered by Alberta Health Care Insurance (AHCIP).

## How to book

Use the rejuvenation online booking, or call 403-398-5449 when the clinic is open. Online requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance.
