## What skin tags are

Skin tags are small, soft growths of skin. They are common and usually harmless.

## See a doctor first if a growth changes

Have a doctor look at any skin growth that changes in size, colour or shape, bleeds, or is painful before you have it removed.

## Laser treatment

The clinic also lists cherry angiomas and skin tags among the conditions treated with laser. See [Rejuvenation and aesthetics](/rejuvenation/).

## Coverage and booking

Skin tag removal is not covered by Alberta Health Care Insurance (AHCIP). Book a medical appointment online, or call 403-398-5449 when the clinic is open. Online requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance.
