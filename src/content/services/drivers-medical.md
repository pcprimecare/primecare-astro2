## What a driver's medical is

A driver's medical is a medical examination by a doctor. The doctor completes a report about whether you are medically fit to drive. Some drivers are asked for this report when they apply for or renew a driver's licence, or when a health condition may affect driving.

## Coverage

A driver's medical is not covered by Alberta Health Care Insurance (AHCIP).

## How to book

Book a medical appointment online, or call 403-398-5449 when the clinic is open. Online requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance. Not all appointment types can be booked online.
