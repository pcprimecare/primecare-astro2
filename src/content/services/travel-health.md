## Travel advice

Travel advice is health advice before you travel. The health risks depend on where you are going, how long you will be away and your own health. Ask for an appointment before your trip.

## Vaccinations other than influenza

The clinic gives vaccines other than the influenza (flu) vaccine. Vaccines other than influenza are not covered by Alberta Health Care Insurance (AHCIP).

## How to book

Book a medical appointment online, or call 403-398-5449 when the clinic is open. Online requests must be made at least 2 business days in advance. Not all appointment types can be booked online.
